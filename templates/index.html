How the check works

  1. The photo is resized and cleaned up.
  2. Colour, edge, texture and fine-detail measurements are extracted with OpenCV.
  3. A scikit-learn model scores how closely they match genuine notes.
  4. Low-confidence or blurry results are flagged for manual verification instead of guessing.
